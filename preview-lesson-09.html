Soft Gel Tips
& Proper Sizing

A good fit begins with observation—not guesswork. Learn to choose tips that follow the natural nail comfortably and consistently.

Learn. Practice. Perfect.

Understand the tip. Check the fit. Record every size.

01 · YOUR LEARNING GOALS

By the end of this lesson…

  • Explain what a full-cover soft gel tip is.
  • Distinguish full-cover tips, partial-well tips and sculpted extensions.
  • Check width, sidewall coverage, curvature and contact.
  • Recognise a tip that is too narrow, too wide or poorly matched.
  • Pre-size and record all ten tips individually.
  • Explain why product compatibility and manufacturer instructions matter.
02 · UNDERSTAND THE OPTIONS

Three ways to create length

These extension methods are different. Their fit, contact area, materials and application steps are not interchangeable.

Full-cover soft gel tip

Pre-shaped tip covers most or all of the nail plate using its compatible adhesive system.

Partial-well tip

Attaches at a smaller contact area near the free edge and is finished according to its system.

Sculpted form

Enhancement product is built over a form positioned beneath the natural free edge.

Remember: a tip that looks beautiful in the box is not automatically the right fit for a particular nail.
03 · ASSESS BEFORE YOU SELECT

Read the natural nail first

Observe the nail before comparing any tip. Look at its natural contours and condition without forcing or pressing on it.

  • Overall width and sidewall shape.
  • Transverse curve across the nail.
  • Length and condition of the free edge.
  • Whether the nail is relatively flat, curved or asymmetrical.
  • Surrounding skin and any visible damage or irritation.
If the nail is injured or shows a concerning change, postpone the service and follow appropriate professional guidance.
04 · WIDTH & SIDEWALL COVERAGE

Choose the correct width

A suitable tip should cover the intended nail area from sidewall to sidewall without pressure, pinching or rocking.

TOO NARROW

Edges may remain uncovered. Do not stretch or force the tip.

TOO WIDE

May overhang. Tailor only if the manufacturer permits it.

FIT CHECK

Coverage follows the nail contours without pinching or rocking.

Tip numbers vary by brand and range. Compare the actual tip to the nail—not the number alone.
