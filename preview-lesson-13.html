Infill
& Maintenance

Learn how to assess an existing enhancement, decide whether it is suitable for maintenance and perform a careful, system-approved infill service.

Learn. Practice. Perfect.

Assess first. Maintain safely. Restore balance.

01 · YOUR LEARNING GOALS

By the end of this lesson…

  • Explain what an infill service is and when it may be appropriate.
  • Assess the existing enhancement and natural nail before maintenance.
  • Recognise lifting, cracks, damage and signs that require removal instead.
  • Describe a safe, system-led infill sequence.
  • Explain aftercare and maintenance timing without promising a fixed schedule for everyone.
An infill is not simply adding product over old product. Assessment determines whether maintenance is appropriate.
02 · WHAT IS AN INFILL?

Understanding maintenance

An infill or rebalance service addresses suitable existing enhancements as the natural nail grows.

  • New natural nail growth becomes visible near the proximal area.
  • The enhancement may need its balance and shape restored.
  • Existing product must be checked for adhesion and integrity.
  • Only compatible, approved products and procedures should be used.
03 · ASSESS BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Check the existing set

  • Ask about discomfort, impact, picking or changes since the last service.
  • Inspect every nail for lifting, cracks, separation, discolouration or damage.
  • Check the natural nail and surrounding skin.
  • Review the product system and confirm the planned maintenance is permitted.
  • Decide whether to proceed, modify the service or remove the enhancement safely.
Do not perform an infill over lifting, trapped debris, compromised product or a potentially infectious concern. Pause and follow appropriate safety guidance.
04 · INFILL OR REMOVE?

Choose the appropriate service

MAINTENANCE MAY BE SUITABLE

Enhancement is intact, properly adhered, compatible with the system and the natural nail is healthy enough for the planned service.

STOP & REASSESS

There is lifting, cracking, suspected contamination, damage, discomfort or an issue that cannot be safely corrected within the system instructions.

When in doubt, do not cover the problem. Follow the manufacturer's removal guidance and refer concerns outside your professional scope.
05 · PREPARATION

Prepare the service safely

  • Clean and sanitise the workstation and tools according to local requirements.
  • Perform hand hygiene and follow appropriate client and technician protection measures.
  • Inspect the natural nail and enhancement again before filing.
  • Remove only the product required by the approved maintenance procedure.
  • Use suitable abrasives and controlled technique; avoid thinning or damaging the natural nail.
Never file over an area that appears infected, painful or compromised. Stop and recommend appropriate healthcare advice when indicated.
06 · REMOVE OLD PRODUCT

Refine without over-filing

  • Follow the product system's specific infill or rebalance instructions.
  • Reduce bulk and remove lifted or unsuitable material using the approved method.
  • Do not force, pry or peel product from the nail.
  • Keep the natural nail plate protected.
  • Remove dust and prepare the surface as directed by the system.
07 · RESTORE THE STRUCTURE

Rebalance for the current nail

Natural nail growth changes the enhancement's proportions over time.

  • Reassess length, shape, alignment and balance.
  • Use the system-approved product and application method.
  • Build the intended structure without excessive bulk.
  • Keep product away from skin and surrounding tissue.
  • Follow the correct curing instructions for the product and lamp combination.
Do not assume the original structure can simply be copied. Reassess each nail and follow the system's current maintenance protocol.
08 · FINISH & INSPECT

Check the completed maintenance

  • Check the top view, side profile and free edge.
  • Confirm smooth transitions and appropriate balance.
  • Check that no product touches the skin.
  • Look for cracks, gaps, lifting or visible defects.
  • Complete the finish and aftercare steps required by the system.
09 · CLIENT AFTERCARE

Help the enhancement wear well

  • Follow the aftercare guidance for the specific product system.
  • Avoid using nails as tools, picking or forcefully removing product.
  • Wear suitable gloves for cleaning or prolonged water and chemical exposure where appropriate.
  • Use recommended cuticle care without applying products in a way that affects adhesion.
  • Contact the technician if lifting, cracks, discomfort or damage develops.
Maintenance timing varies by product system, nail growth, lifestyle and condition. Recommend a suitable interval based on assessment—not a universal promise.
10 · PRACTICE ACTIVITY

Make the maintenance decision

Use a practice hand, training tip or fictional client case.

  1. Review an example enhancement and list what you would inspect.
  2. Identify any signs that would make you pause or decline an infill.
  3. Write the safe sequence you would follow for a suitable maintenance service.
  4. List the product and lamp instructions you would verify.
  5. Create a short aftercare explanation and maintenance recommendation.
Practice goal

Decide whether to maintain before deciding how to maintain.
