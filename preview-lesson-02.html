Nail Hygiene,
Sanitation & Disinfection

Learn the hygiene habits that protect both nail technicians and clients.

Understand

Cleaning, sanitising and disinfecting.

Prepare

A clean, organised workstation.

Protect

Reusable tools and single-use supplies.

Prevent

Cross-contamination during services.

01 · WHY IT MATTERS

Hygiene is part of every service

A professional nail service begins before any product is opened. Hands, tools, surfaces, products and waste routines all matter.

Remember: A beautiful result is never more important than health and safety.

A simple hygiene cycle: prepare the station, perform hand hygiene, use appropriately processed tools, keep clean and used items separate, discard single-use supplies, and reset the workstation.

02 · THREE IMPORTANT TERMS

Cleaning, sanitising & disinfecting

Cleaning

Removes visible dust, dirt, product residue and other material.

Sanitising

Reduces microorganisms to a level considered safer under an applicable standard.

Disinfecting

Uses an appropriate process to inactivate specified microorganisms on compatible items.

Clean first. Residue can interfere with disinfection. Always follow the product label and local requirements.
03 · HAND HYGIENE

Protect yourself and your client

Perform hand hygiene before and after each client, after handling used tools or waste, after removing gloves, and whenever hands are visibly dirty.

  • Use soap and running water when hands are visibly soiled.
  • Use a suitable hand rub only as directed.
  • Gloves do not replace hand hygiene.
  • Cover cuts with a suitable waterproof dressing.
04 · WORKSTATION RESET

Prepare before the client arrives

Clean the surface, use an appropriate disinfectant when required, and observe its full label-specified contact time.

Keep ready

Protected clean tools, clean barriers and only the supplies needed.

Keep separate

Used tools, waste, food, drinks and personal belongings away from the clean work area.

General education only. Follow local public-health requirements, product labels and manufacturer instructions.
