01 · THE FOUNDATION

Understanding the natural nail

The natural nail is made mainly of layers of hardened keratin. The visible nail plate grows from specialised tissue in the nail unit.

Nail plate

The hard, visible keratin structure commonly called the nail.

Nail bed

The living tissue beneath the nail plate that supports it.

Nail matrix

The growth area that produces the cells forming the nail plate.

Lunula

The pale half-moon area sometimes visible near the base of the nail; it is the visible part of the distal matrix.

02 · IDENTIFY THE PARTS

Main nail structures

Nail plate · visible hard surfaceFree edge · beyond fingertipLunula · pale half-moonNail bed · beneath plate
At the base

The matrix sits beneath the proximal nail fold and produces the cells that form the nail plate. The lunula may be visible as a pale half-moon.

Along the sides

The lateral nail folds border the nail plate. The nail bed lies underneath the plate, while the free edge extends past the fingertip.

This simplified top-view guide highlights visible landmarks. Some structures, including the matrix and nail bed, are beneath the surface and cannot be fully seen from above. It is a learning aid, not a clinical diagram.

03 · PROTECTIVE SKIN

Know the difference

Eponychium

Living skin at the proximal nail fold that helps form a protective seal at the base of the nail.

Cuticle

Thin, non-living tissue that can remain attached to the nail plate as it grows.

Proximal nail fold

The fold of skin at the base of the nail.

Lateral nail folds

The skin folds along the sides of the nail plate.

Safety first: Do not cut living skin or force tools under the nail fold. If the skin is cut or bleeding occurs, stop the service and follow appropriate first-aid and hygiene procedures.
04 · EVERYDAY LANGUAGE

Essential nail-tech terminology

Preparation

Steps taken to prepare the natural nail before product application.

Dehydrator

A product used to temporarily remove surface moisture and oil.

Primer

A product used, when required, to support product adhesion.

Base coat

A foundational coating applied before colour or enhancement product, as directed by the system.
