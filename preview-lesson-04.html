Natural Nail
Preparation

Learn how to prepare the natural nail gently, safely and consistently before a gel service.

Assess

Check the nail and surrounding skin before starting.

Shape

Refine the free edge with light, controlled filing.

Protect

Understand living skin and non-living cuticle.

Prepare

Follow the product system's instructions.

01 · ASSESS FIRST

Look before you begin

Observe the natural nail and surrounding skin. Ask about sensitivities, allergies and previous product reactions.

Pause or decline if you see broken skin, swelling, pain, unusual discolouration or other concerning changes. Do not diagnose; recommend an appropriate healthcare professional when needed.

Confirm the service is suitable before preparing the nail.

02 · SHAPE GENTLY

Refine the free edge

Use a suitable file and light, controlled strokes. Respect the client's preferred shape and the natural nail's condition.

Use light pressure

Abrasive choice and technique should suit natural nails.

Avoid damage

Do not aggressively saw, force the file into sidewalls or thin the nail plate.

03 · CUTICLE AREA

Know what is living

The cuticle is non-living tissue attached to the nail plate. The eponychium is living skin at the proximal nail fold.

Only address non-living cuticle where appropriate and within your training. Never cut living skin, dig under the fold or continue through pain.

If you cannot clearly distinguish the tissue, leave it alone. Safe preparation does not require aggressive cuticle removal.

04 · PREPARE THE SURFACE

Gentle, system-approved steps

Some gel systems require gentle removal of surface shine; others specify a different method. Follow the exact product instructions.

Remove dust

Use a clean brush or lint-free wipe.

Use correct products

Apply cleanser, dehydrator or primer only as directed.

Never over-file or aggressively buff the natural nail. Avoid skin contact with nail products and keep the prepared surface clean.
