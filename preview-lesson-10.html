Fit · Alignment · Product control · Compatibility · Inspection

02 · YOUR LEARNING GOALS

What you'll learn

By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to:

  • Distinguish full-cover tips, partial-well tips and sculpted forms.
  • Choose a method suited to the natural nail, requested length and product system.
  • Explain preparation, fit, support, product control and curing checkpoints.
  • Recognise gaps, bubbles, skin contact and alignment faults.
  • Describe a methodical, system-specific workflow.
  • Inspect an extension and know when to stop or reassess.
This lesson is a theory and practice framework—not a substitute for supervised hands-on training.
03 · UNDERSTAND THE METHODS

Three ways to create length

FULL-COVER TIP

Pre-shaped tip

Covers most or all of the nail plate and attaches with its compatible adhesive system.

PARTIAL-WELL TIP

Smaller contact area

Attaches near the free edge, then is blended and overlaid as the system directs.

SCULPTED FORM

Built over a form

A form supports enhancement product while the extension is sculpted.

These methods are not interchangeable. Preparation, contact area, product requirements and removal can differ.

04 · METHOD SELECTION

Choose the method—not just the look

Match the extension method to the natural nail, the service goal and the product system.

  • Natural nail condition and free-edge length.
  • Desired extension length and shape.
  • Natural nail width and curve.
  • Product compatibility and your training.
  • Client's everyday activities and maintenance expectations.
Postpone the service if the nail is unsuitable or shows damage or irritation that requires attention. Never promise a fixed wear time.
05 · PLAN BEFORE APPLICATION

Prepare the service thoughtfully

Client & nail assessment

  • Observe the nail plate and free edge.
  • Agree on a realistic length and shape.
  • Consider lifestyle and maintenance.
  • Explain the method and aftercare.

Technician preparation

  • Read the chosen manufacturer's guide.
  • Confirm products and lamp compatibility.
  • Arrange tools and materials for control.
  • Know when to pause or decline a service.
A longer extension is not automatically a better result. Suitability and comfort matter.
06 · PREPARATION & FIT

Check the setup before attaching

Preparation must follow the selected product system. Do not copy another brand's sequence.

  • Use clean hands and tools.
  • Prepare the natural nail only as directed.
  • For tips, confirm size and contact fit.
  • For forms, confirm secure placement without unintended gaps.
  • Check sidewalls and centreline alignment.
Never force or pinch a tip or form to make it fit. Reassess the setup before proceeding.
07 · CONTROLLED PRODUCT PLACEMENT

Work with control and pause points

Align
Position the tip or form according to the chosen system.
Place
Use only the specified product and application method.
Inspect contact
Check for movement, gaps or trapped air before continuing.
Pause if compromised
Correct the setup using the manufacturer's instructions.
Keep product away from surrounding skin and cuticle area. Do not cure a visibly compromised application just to save time.

The exact placement technique is product-system specific; this sequence is a checkpoint framework.

08 · CURING & COMPATIBILITY

One lamp does not fit every gel

  • Follow the exact cure schedule and application thickness.
  • Do not judge full cure by surface appearance alone.
  • Do not mix products unless compatibility is confirmed.
  • Keep uncured product off the skin.
  • Follow product safety guidance if unusual heat or discomfort occurs.
Never use universal cure seconds. Curing depends on the formulation and validated lamp system.
09 · REFINE & INSPECT

Look from more than one angle

Inspection checkpoints

  • Alignment with the finger.
  • Shape and symmetry from above.
  • Side profile and transitions.
  • Free-edge thickness and finish.
  • Cuticle and sidewall area.
  • Natural nail protection.
Do not file aggressively to disguise a poor fit. If an extension is unstable, painful or visibly compromised, stop and reassess.

Simplified inspection viewpoints; not a substitute for hands-on demonstration.

10 · COMMON APPLICATION PROBLEMS

Recognise the fault. Pause and reassess.

GAP OR AIR POCKET

Contact problem

Recheck size, curvature and the system's contact requirements. Follow its correction or reapplication instructions.

OFF-CENTRE TIP / FORM

Alignment problem

Reposition before proceeding, following the system instructions.

PRODUCT ON SKIN

Safety concern

Remove uncured product safely before curing. Never cure product on skin.

BULK OR LIFTING

Structure concern

Investigate fit, preparation, compatibility, curing and aftercare. Do not assume one cause.

Do not hide gaps with extra adhesive or cure over a known application fault.
11 · PRACTICE & PROFESSIONAL CHECKLIST

Practice with intention

Practice workflow

  1. Compare the three methods and choose one for a practice scenario.
  2. Read the selected manufacturer's guide.
  3. Check tip fit or form placement.
  4. Rehearse on a training hand or practice tip—not a paying client.
  5. Inspect top, side and front views.
  6. Record one strength and one improvement area.

Before proceeding

  • Method suits the nail and service.
  • Tip fit or form placement checked.
  • Product and lamp compatible.
  • Exact prep and cure instructions followed.
  • No uncured product cured on skin.
  • Extension aligned and inspected.
Key takeaway: Extension quality begins before product application. Planning, fit, controlled placement, compatible curing and careful inspection all matter.
