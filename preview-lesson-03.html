01 · BUILD YOUR TOOLKIT

Start with the essentials

A nail technician does not need every tool on the market. Begin with a small, reliable kit that suits the services you offer.

Choose tools you understand, can maintain safely, and know how to use without damaging the natural nail.

Organise your kit for preparation, application, curing, finishing, hygiene and storage.

02 · CORE MANICURE TOOLS

Know what each tool does

Nail file

Shapes the free edge. Choose a suitable grit and use light pressure.

Buffer

Refines a surface when appropriate. Over-buffing can weaken the nail.

Cuticle pusher

Used gently on non-living cuticle only; never force it under living skin.

Dust brush

Removes loose filing dust and must be kept clean.

03 · EQUIPMENT

Set up a safe workspace

A stable table, comfortable chair, suitable task lighting, dust-control arrangements, covered storage and a lined waste bin support an organised service.

An electric file is optional specialist equipment—not a beginner requirement. It needs proper training and controlled technique.

Keep electrical devices in good condition and away from liquids. Maintain equipment according to its manufacturer's instructions.

04 · GEL PRODUCT BASICS

Understand your product system

Dehydrator

Temporarily removes surface moisture and oil when required.

Primer / bonder

Supports adhesion when recommended by the system.

Base coat

Foundation layer used under gel colour as directed.

Gel polish & top coat

Colour and finishing layers that must be applied and cured as instructed.

Products from different systems are not automatically compatible. Follow manufacturer directions.
