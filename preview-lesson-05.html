Understanding Gel
Products & Curing

Discover how gel products work, why lamp compatibility matters, and how to handle products safely.

Identify

Understand base, colour, top and structure gels.

Match

Use a lamp approved for the gel system.

Apply

Work in thin, even layers as directed.

Protect

Prevent skin contact with uncured gel.

01 · KNOW YOUR PRODUCTS

Every gel has a purpose

Base coat, colour gel, top coat, builder gel and gel adhesive are designed for different roles.

Base coat

Creates the foundation when the system calls for it.

Colour and top

Add colour and the specified finish.

Product names do not make formulas interchangeable. Follow the complete system instructions.
02 · LAMP COMPATIBILITY

Not every lamp cures every gel

The gel and lamp must be compatible with the light requirements stated by the manufacturer.

Do not assume that any UV or LED lamp is suitable. Check the approved lamp guidance, wavelength range and curing time for the exact product.

A bright lamp is not proof of compatibility or complete curing.

03 · APPLY IN THIN LAYERS

Follow the curing instructions

Thick layers, dense pigments, incorrect hand placement or an unsuitable lamp can affect curing.

Thin, even coats

Use the layer thickness recommended by the maker.

Full exposure

Position the hand correctly and use the full instructed time.

Never shorten curing time because the surface feels hard. Do not cure gel while it touches skin.
04 · SKIN SAFETY

Keep uncured gel off the skin

Uncured gel can contain reactive ingredients. Repeated skin contact can increase the risk of sensitisation.

Remove accidental skin contact safely before curing. Never place a hand in the lamp while gel remains on the skin. Stop if a client reports burning, itching or an unusual reaction.

Follow product instructions and refer concerning symptoms to an appropriate healthcare professional. Do not diagnose.
