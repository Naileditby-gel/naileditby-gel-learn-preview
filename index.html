WELCOME — WE’RE GLAD YOU’RE HERE

A learning space for your nail-tech journey

Welcome to Naileditby_gel Learn—a friendly learning space for aspiring nail technicians and nail artists who want to strengthen their skills. Our lessons are designed to make nail-tech knowledge easier to understand through clear explanations, visual guides, practical activities and short knowledge checks. Start with the foundations of natural nail anatomy, preparation and hygiene, then build toward gel polish application, clean finishing and creative nail design. Learn one topic at a time, practise at your own pace, and keep improving with every step.
