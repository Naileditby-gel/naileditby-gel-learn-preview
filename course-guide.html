Course Guide
& Lesson Index

A clear roadmap of the Beginner and Intermediate learning paths, with a short overview of every lesson so students can see what they will learn and how each topic connects.

How to use this guide

Follow lessons in order. Complete the explanations, visual guides, practice activities and knowledge checks. Revisit earlier topics when you need a refresher. Practical techniques should also be practised under suitable supervision and according to product instructions.
